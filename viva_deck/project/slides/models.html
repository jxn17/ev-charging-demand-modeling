Deviance is the right accuracy score for counts; MAE rewards predicting the median, which is zero in sparse hours, which is why the booster looks good on MAE while predicting 68.1% zeros against 61.1% observed. Count-model 90% coverage is above 90% because integer quantiles make the intervals conservative. Daily level: GLM daily MAE 3.64 sessions on a mean of 16.2 per site-day (MAPE 28.3%) vs OLS 3.59 (30.9%), seasonal naive 4.03 (34.2%) and HGB 5.34 (32.3%). Honesty point: on 2019, a model with no trend at all would have scored slightly better (deviance 0.7723 vs 0.7781), but we kept the validation-based choice so we never selected on the test set.
