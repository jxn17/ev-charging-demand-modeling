Motivation

The problem and four research gaps

Chargers are sited reactively, not from measured demand.

The result: crowded garages next to idle ones, long waits, and slower EV adoption where demand is highest.

1 · Interpretability vs accuracy

Ours: count GLMs read as rate ratios, benchmarked against Poisson gradient boosting.

2 · Forecasting cut off from siting

Ours: forecasts feed a gap map, DBSCAN and a queueing simulation in one pipeline.

3 · Counts treated as continuous

Ours: Poisson and NB2 likelihoods with a port-exposure offset, compared with OLS.

4 · Little density-based clustering

Ours: DBSCAN on high-gap grid cells, with ε read from a k-distance plot.
