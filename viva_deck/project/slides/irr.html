How to read an IRR: exp(beta) multiplies the expected arrival rate per active port, holding everything else fixed. Weekday 9 am at Bryant: about 228 times the midnight rate. The lag terms are elasticities: doubling (1 + the trailing 7-day same-hour mean) multiplies the rate by 2^0.29 = 1.22. Confidence intervals are cluster-robust by site-day, because hours in the same day share shocks. The site-specific fee effects are linear combinations of the fee main effect and site x fee terms, with CIs from the full covariance matrix. Caveat for Ted Thompson and Cambridge: in March 2018, after the fee, they went from 2 to 8 and from 4 to 10 ports, so their fee effect is partly confounded with the expansion.
